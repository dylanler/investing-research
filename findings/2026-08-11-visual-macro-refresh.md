## Macro read-through

The operating backdrop remains constructive for AI infrastructure, but less forgiving of duration and execution risk. The Federal Reserve held its policy rate at 3.5%–3.75% on July 29 while describing inflation as elevated. June CPI fell 0.4% month over month but remained 3.5% higher year over year. The IEA expects global electricity demand to grow 3.6% in 2026 and 3.8% in 2027, with data centers providing a durable structural demand source even as Middle East energy-price shocks add uncertainty.

The practical portfolio implication is to retain exposure to packaging, thermal management, power, passives, and grid-enabling equipment while applying a larger penalty to highly volatile, low-capitalization names. Thesis conviction and rank are deliberately separated: a strong secular thesis does not erase entry-price or volatility risk.
